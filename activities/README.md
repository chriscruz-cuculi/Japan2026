# Activities

## Purpose
This folder contains planning and documentation for all activities, attractions, experiences, and points of interest for the Japan 2026 trip.

## Objective
- Research and curate a list of must-see attractions and experiences
- Book time-sensitive activities and tours in advance
- Organize daily itineraries by city and region
- Track activity costs and booking confirmations
- Compile practical information (hours, directions, tips)

## Plan
1. **Research Phase**
   - Identify top attractions in each destination
   - Research seasonal events and festivals (cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, etc.)
   - Explore cultural experiences:
     - Temples and shrines
     - Museums and galleries
     - Traditional tea ceremonies
     - Cooking classes
     - Sumo tournaments
   - Discover outdoor activities (hiking, hot springs/onsen)
   - Food experiences (restaurants, food tours, markets)
   - Day trips and excursions

2. **Categorization**
   - Cultural & Historical sites
   - Nature & Outdoor activities
   - Food & Dining experiences
   - Entertainment & Nightlife
   - Shopping districts
   - Day trips from major cities

3. **Booking Phase**
   - Reserve tickets for time-sensitive attractions:
     - Ghibli Museum
     - TeamLab exhibitions
     - Popular restaurants (via Tabelog, Google, or hotel concierge)
     - Sumo matches (if timing aligns)
   - Book guided tours or experiences
   - Purchase attraction passes (JR Pass, city passes, etc.)

4. **Organization Phase**
   - Create daily itineraries with realistic timing
   - Map out activities by geographic proximity
   - Note opening hours, closed days, and admission fees
   - Include transportation directions to each location
   - Mark priorities (must-do vs. optional)
   - Build in flexibility and rest time

5. **Documentation**
   - Store booking confirmations
   - Save venue contact information
   - Keep digital and physical copies of tickets
   - Note any dress codes or etiquette requirements
   - Collect recommendations from travel guides and locals
