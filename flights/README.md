# Flights

## Purpose
This folder contains all information and documentation related to flight bookings and air travel for the Japan 2026 trip.

## Objective
- Research and compare flight options to Japan
- Track flight prices and availability
- Store booking confirmations and itineraries
- Document domestic flights within Japan (if needed)
- Maintain records of travel insurance and flight-related documentation

## Plan
1. **Research Phase**
   - Identify departure and arrival airports
   - Compare airlines (direct vs. connecting flights)
   - Determine optimal travel dates based on seasonality and pricing
   - Research baggage allowances and airline policies

2. **Booking Phase**
   - Monitor flight prices using price trackers
   - Book international flights to/from Japan
   - Book any domestic flights within Japan (e.g., Tokyo to Osaka, Sapporo)
   - Purchase travel insurance if needed

3. **Documentation Phase**
   - Store e-tickets and booking confirmations
   - Save airline contact information
   - Document check-in procedures and requirements
   - Note flight change/cancellation policies

4. **Pre-Travel Phase**
   - Online check-in 24 hours before departure
   - Download airline apps
   - Verify passport validity and visa requirements
   - Confirm baggage limits and prepare accordingly
