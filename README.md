# Japan Trip 2025: Strategic Planning Guide

A comprehensive travel planning document for a group trip to Japan during peak cherry blossom season (April 3-11, 2025).

## Overview

This project contains a strategic itinerary and logistics brief for a 7-person group trip to Japan, optimized for:
- **Peak Sakura (Cherry Blossom) Season** viewing
- **Cost-effective transportation** (saves $145/person vs JR Pass)
- **Family-friendly accommodation** in apartment hotels
- **Balanced pacing** suitable for mixed ages

## Key Features

### 🏨 Accommodation Strategy
- **Solution**: MIMARU Apartment Hotels (40m²-80m² units)
- **Configuration**: 2-bedroom suites with kitchenettes, dining areas, and bunk beds
- **Target Locations**:
  - MIMARU Tokyo Ueno North (near airport & Ueno Park)
  - MIMARU Tokyo Asakusa Station (historic district)
  - MIMARU Kyoto Station (bullet train access)

### 🗺️ The "Sakura Chase" Itinerary (8 Days)

#### Tokyo (April 3-7)
- Day 1: Arrival & settling in
- Day 2: Senso-ji Temple + Ueno Park (prime Sakura viewing)
- Day 3: teamLab Planets + Shibuya & Harajuku
- Day 4: Free day (DisneySea option or gardens)

#### Kyoto (April 7-11)
- Day 5: Bullet train journey + Gion evening walk
- Day 6: Fushimi Inari Shrine + Kiyomizu-dera Temple
- Day 7: Nara day trip (Deer Park + Todai-ji)
- Day 8: Arashiyama Bamboo Grove + Sagano Train
- Day 9: Departure from Osaka

### 💰 Cost Optimization

**JR Pass Analysis** (Counterintuitive Finding):
- 7-Day JR Pass: ¥50,000 ($330 USD)
- Individual tickets: ¥28,000 ($185 USD)
- **Savings: $145 per person** by NOT buying the pass

### 🧳 Travel Hacks

1. **Open Jaw Flights**: Fly into Tokyo, out of Osaka (saves 4+ hours & ¥20,000)
2. **Takkyubin Luggage Service**: Ship bags between hotels (~$15/bag, arrives next day)
3. **SmartEx App**: Book Shinkansen tickets 30 days in advance
4. **Early Morning Visits**: Beat crowds at major sites (e.g., Fushimi Inari at 8 AM)

## Cherry Blossom Forecast 2025

- **Tokyo**: Full bloom March 31 - April 5 (catching the "hanafubuki" petal fall)
- **Kyoto**: Full bloom April 4 - 10 (absolute peak timing)
